Privacy Policy — Rivals

Last updated: 2026-05-26

Rivals ("we", "us") operates the Rivals mobile and web application (the "Service"). This page explains what data we collect, why, and the rights you have over it.

1. Data we collect

Account data — email, username, display name, optional avatar URL, and hashed password (or OAuth provider id).

Group and habit data — groups, habits, daily logs, streaks, leaderboard scores.

Proof photos — encrypted at rest in Cloudflare R2; visible only to group members.

Device data — push token, platform, app + OS version.

Telemetry — anonymised events to PostHog and Sentry. No photos or habit text in telemetry.

2. Why we collect it

  • To operate the Service.
  • To deliver opted-in notifications.
  • To detect bugs and abuse.
  • To measure aggregate usage.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

3. Where it lives

  • Database (Supabase Postgres) — EU region.
  • Photos (Cloudflare R2) — auto-region.
  • Telemetry (PostHog, Sentry) — per their policies.

4. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Access / portability — Profile → Privacy → Export.
  • Deletion — Profile → Privacy → Delete. R2 photos purged within 30 days.
  • Correction — edit profile fields any time.
  • Opt-out — disable analytics in Profile.

5. Retention

Account data is kept while active. Backups roll off within 30 days after deletion.

6. Children

Not intended for users under 13. We delete data we learn belongs to a child under 13.
